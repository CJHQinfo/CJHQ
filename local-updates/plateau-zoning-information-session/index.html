CJHQ Borough Updates - Plateau-Mont-Royal

PLATEAU ZONING CHANGES - PUBLIC INFORMATION SESSION TOMORROW

Dear Community Members,

The Plateau-Mont-Royal borough is holding a public information session tomorrow, Friday, November 20, regarding proposed changes to its zoning regulations. These changes could affect homeowners, property owners, businesses and community institutions.

Public Information Session - Tomorrow, November 20

The borough is holding an open house where residents can ask questions and discuss the proposed changes directly with borough representatives.

Date: Friday, November 20, 2026
Time: 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.
Location: 201 ‌Avenue Laurier East

Residents who cannot attend can also submit questions or comments online until December 1, 2026.

Submit questions or comments online

What is this about?

In 2025, the City of Montréal, under the previous Projet Montréal administration, adopted a long-term plan called PUM 2050, outlining how the city should develop over the coming decades, including housing, buildings, transportation and neighbourhood development.

Each borough must now update its local zoning regulations to follow this plan. These regulations determine what property owners are allowed to build, how buildings can be expanded and how properties can be used.

What changes are being proposed?

Some of the proposals include:

•  Building heights: Allowing taller buildings in certain areas while maintaining existing height limits in others.
•  Combining apartments: Stricter rules for reducing the number of apartments in a building, which could affect homeowners wishing to combine units into larger homes.
•  Basements: Prohibiting the creation of new living spaces below ground level, while allowing existing developed basements to remain.
•  Property renovations and development: New requirements regarding greenery, paved surfaces, bicycle parking and other aspects of construction.
•  Property uses: Changes to which types of activities and establishments are permitted in different areas.

These changes are still being discussed and have not yet been finalized.
